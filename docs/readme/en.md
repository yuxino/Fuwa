## Pinned views

- **Follow Original** keeps the picture aligned with its source as it moves or resizes. Click through the picture to use the app underneath.
- **Reference Window** lets you move and resize the picture independently or crop it to a selected area. Choose this mode from the gear in Fuwa’s window list.

## Install and use

1. [Download Fuwa](https://github.com/yuxino/fuwa/releases/latest), open the `.dmg`, and drag Fuwa into Applications. Requires macOS 14 or later.
2. Allow Screen Recording when prompted on your first pin. Window images stay on your Mac.
3. Bring the window you want to pin to the front and press `⌥⌘P`. To unpin it, point at the pinned picture and press the shortcut again.
